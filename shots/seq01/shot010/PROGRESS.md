`seq1-scene1_v001.hipnc` (Houdini 22.0.429 Apprentice). A procedural phoenix wing built from the reference painting (`ref/feather_flow_ref.png`), generated by `build_phoenix.py`.

![Final frame](review/oil_paint_render.png)

### What's in this version

- **Feather flows** — 9 guide curves traced from the painting; 4 overlapping feather layers (primaries, secondaries, coverts, down) copied along them. Attribute Randomize drives length, width, bend, overlap and rotation; VEX noise roughens edges and shafts.
- **Color** — teal → green → peach → red ramp driven by canvas position, flow position, root → tip and noise, with per-layer tints.
- **Oil-paint look** — Karma MaterialX shader: strokes along the feather barbs (via feather UVs), impasto bump, dry-brush edges over a dark ground, glossy/darker wet paint at the growing front, painted umber/oxblood canvas behind.
- **Oil-run growth** — frames 1–200. Feathers pour from the flow shoulders and run toward the tips: fast then slowing, in surges, with lobed fronts and swollen heads; the vane spreads sideways after the front passes.
- **Wing beat + wind** — gentle beat about the shoulder (4 s period, tips lag and flex), building up as the wing grows and continuing after it settles. External wind (gusts, drifting turbulence) plus the air from the beat bend the feathers; growing feathers bend more.
- **Render setup** — `/stage`: SOP Import → material bindings → camera, dome + key light → Karma (1070×720, CPU, 12 spp) → `paint_render`, writing `$HIP/render/feather_paint.$F4.png`.
- Added by hand in the scene: `cam1`, `distantlight1`, `gilight1`.
